Who We Are

Youth for Empowerment and Solidarity (YES) is a youth-led organization committed to empowering young people across Liberia through education, innovation, leadership, creativity, and community development. We believe that young people are the driving force for national transformation and sustainable progress. Through our programs and initiatives, we create opportunities that equip youths with practical skills, digital knowledge, vocational training, leadership capacity, and platforms to showcase their talents and creativity.

Mission

To Build a Generation of Empowered, skilled and Globally competitive young creative-digital innovators who drives social change, economic growth and national development.

Vision Statement

To Identify young talents, equip them with practical digital and creative skills and connect them to mentorship, platform and opportunities that turn creativity into sustainabl careers.

Our Story

Youth for Empowerment and Solidarity (YES) was founded on the belief that young people aren't just the leaders of tomorrow—they are the change-makers of today. By rooted ourselves in unity and branching out through action, we bridge the gap between passion and impact.
